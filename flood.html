Scenario: Flood

Monsoon and flash floods: evacuate early, avoid moving water, and stay safe during and after inundation.

Scenarios Last reviewed 2026-10-19 8 min read

Floods are India's most common disaster. Most deaths are from driving or walking through moving water and from electrocution. Water is stronger and dirtier than it looks.

Before the water arrives

  • Monitor IMD and CWC forecasts; heed orange and red warnings.
  • Move to higher floors early; do not wait for the water to reach the stairs.
  • Raise furniture, electricals and documents; shut off power at the mains if safe.
  • Fill containers with clean water before contamination.
  • Prepare a go-bag and move vehicles to high ground.
  • Turn off gas and electricity if instructed.

During a flood

Moving water

Never walk, swim or drive through flood water. 15 cm of moving water can knock you down; 30 cm can move a car. Turn around, don't drown.

  • Move to the highest safe floor; avoid the attic unless you can reach the roof.
  • Do not touch electrical equipment or wiring while wet.
  • Avoid bridges, underpasses and riverbanks.
  • Drink only treated water; flood water contaminates everything it touches.
  • Watch for snakes and insects displaced by water.
  • Signal for help with a torch or bright cloth if trapped.

After the flood

  • Wait for official clearance before returning.
  • Do not enter a building with structural damage; check for gas leaks and downed lines.
  • Wear boots and gloves; wash hands after contact with flood water.
  • Discard food exposed to flood water, including sealed packages.
  • Disinfect surfaces and boil all drinking water.
  • Photograph damage for insurance before cleaning.
  • Watch for waterborne disease and seek care for fever or diarrhoea.

India-specific notes

  • Monsoon flooding peaks June–September; check local flood forecasts.
  • Urban flooding is common in Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru and Kolkata; underpasses and basements fill fast.
  • Dam releases can cause sudden downstream flooding; follow official warnings.
  • NDRF teams are deployed by state authorities; follow district collector instructions.
  • Keep important documents in a waterproof bag; floods destroy paper quickly.
