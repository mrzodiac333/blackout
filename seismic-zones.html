Seismic Zones of India

Understand India's earthquake zones, assess your risk, and retrofit or plan accordingly.

India Hazards Last reviewed 2026-10-19 5 min read

India is divided into four seismic zones (II–V) by the Bureau of Indian Standards, based on expected shaking. Zone V is the highest risk. Knowing your zone guides construction, insurance and planning.

The zones

Seismic zones of India. Zone I is no longer used.
ZoneRiskRegions
IILowMost of peninsular India, parts of the northeast
IIIModerateKerala, Goa, Mumbai, Kolkata, parts of the Ganga plain
IVHighDelhi, parts of Himachal, Uttarakhand, Bihar, West Bengal, Sikkim, Andaman
VVery highKashmir, Himachal, Uttarakhand, northeast states, Kutch, Andaman & Nicobar

Check your address against the BIS seismic zone map or local municipal building code. Zone IV and V require earthquake-resistant construction.

Assessing your building

  • Older masonry buildings without reinforcement are the highest risk.
  • Look for cracks, tilting walls, heavy roofs and unsupported parapets.
  • Soft-storey buildings (open ground floor for parking) are vulnerable.
  • Get a structural engineer's assessment if you are in Zone IV or V.
  • Retrofit where possible; anchor shelves and water heaters.
  • Keep heavy objects off high shelves and beds.

Preparing for earthquakes

  • Identify safe spots: under sturdy tables, against interior walls, away from windows.
  • Practise drop, cover and hold on with the household.
  • Keep shoes, a torch and a whistle by the bed.
  • Know how to shut off gas and electricity.
  • Keep a go-bag and documents ready.
  • Insure your home and contents.
