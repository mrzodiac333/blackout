Navigation Without GPS

Read a paper map, use a compass, navigate by sun and stars, and plan routes when GPS or phones fail.

Skills & Security Last reviewed 2026-10-19 8 min read

GPS is convenient but fragile. A paper map, a compass and basic skills get you home when batteries die or networks fail. Learn to navigate in your own city first.

Reading a map

  • Scale: 1:50,000 means 1 cm = 500 m. Check the scale bar.
  • North: orient the map so north is up, or use the compass to set it.
  • Contours: closer lines mean steeper ground; V-shapes point upstream.
  • Grid references: read easting then northing; practise with your local map.
  • Landmarks: towers, rivers, railway lines and major roads are reliable; shops and signs change.

Compass basics

  1. Hold the compass level, away from metal and magnets.
  2. Let the needle settle; the red end points magnetic north.
  3. Rotate the bezel to set your bearing.
  4. Turn your body until the orienting arrow aligns with the needle.
  5. Follow the direction-of-travel arrow.

Magnetic declination varies across India; check your map's declination note. A smartphone compass can help but is unreliable near metal and when the battery dies.

Natural navigation

  • Sun: rises roughly east, sets roughly west; at noon it is roughly south in India.
  • Shadows: mark a shadow tip, wait 15 minutes, mark again — the line runs roughly east–west.
  • Stars: Polaris (the North Star) sits nearly due north; find it from the Big Dipper.
  • Moon: not reliable for direction; use it only as a rough guide.
  • Land: moss and moisture are not reliable direction indicators — do not depend on them.

Route planning

  • Plan two routes: a primary and a backup, with printed turn-by-turn notes.
  • Estimate time generously; add 50% for crowds, weather and carrying loads.
  • Identify water, shelter and medical points along the route.
  • Set a turnaround time; tell someone your route and expected arrival.
  • Mark your position on the map as you go; keep the map dry.
  • If lost: stop, think, observe, plan. Do not wander. Follow water downstream to settlements only if safe.
