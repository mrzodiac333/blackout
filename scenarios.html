India's natural hazards

These happen every year. Know your local risk and the early actions that matter.

Official instructions win

Every playbook here is general guidance. If the district collector, NDMA, NDRF, IMD or police issue instructions, follow them over anything on this site.

Read this first: This guide is for education and preparedness. It does not replace professional training, medical care, or local emergency instructions. In a real emergency, follow official guidance and call the numbers on the contacts page. Radio transmission requires a WPC licence in India; medical content is not a prescription.
