Power: Solar, Batteries, Generators

Keep phones, lights and medical devices running during an outage with solar, battery banks and safe generator use.

Survival Last reviewed 2026-10-19 9 min read

Modern survival is mostly about electrons: light, communications, refrigeration for medicine, and pumping water. Plan for a two-week outage. The safest system is small, solar-charged and fuse-protected.

Headlamp2 W3 hours6

Laptop45 W2 hours90
Small fan20 W6 hours120

A 20,000 mAh power bank stores roughly 74 Wh — enough for phones and lights for days, not for a fan or fridge. Use the power calculator to size a system.

Small solar setup

  • Panel: a 50–100 W folding panel can charge a battery bank and run small loads.
  • Controller: MPPT is more efficient; PWM is cheaper. Match controller to panel voltage and battery chemistry.
  • Battery: LiFePO4 is lighter and safer for indoor use; lead-acid is cheaper but needs ventilation.
  • Inverter: only if you need mains AC; expect losses and buy a pure sine-wave unit.
Lithium safety

Use quality cells and a proper BMS. Never charge a cold Li-ion pack, never puncture or crush cells, and never charge unattended on a flammable surface. Swollen cells are a fire risk — isolate and dispose of them safely.

Generators and fuel

  • Run generators outdoors only, at least 5 metres from doors and windows, with the exhaust pointing away.
  • Never store fuel indoors or near a flame; use approved containers and rotate stock.
  • Let the generator cool before refuelling.
  • Use a CO alarm in the house if you run any fuel-burning appliance.
  • Connect appliances directly or use a proper transfer switch — never backfeed a socket.

Making power last

  • Turn off background apps, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth; use airplane mode when not needed.
  • Lower screen brightness and shorten screen timeout.
  • Charge devices during peak solar hours rather than at night.
  • Use one central charging station and keep it off the floor.
  • Prefer headlamps over room lights; light only the space you use.
  • Keep devices cool — heat degrades batteries.
