Ropework & Knots

Six knots that cover most survival needs, plus basic rope care and improvised lashing.

Skills & Security Last reviewed 2026-10-19 6 min read

A handful of knots, practised until they are automatic, is worth more than a bag of fancy hardware. Learn these with a 2 m length of cord today.

Rope care

  • Inspect for cuts, glazing and UV damage; retire compromised rope.
  • Coil loosely and hang; avoid knots stored under tension.
  • Keep synthetic rope away from heat and sharp edges.
  • Wash dirty rope in lukewarm water and dry in shade.
  • Use the right rope: static for hauling, dynamic for climbing loads.

Improvised lashing

  • Square lashing: joins two poles at right angles.
  • Diagonal lashing: braces an X-frame.
  • Shear lashing: makes an A-frame for a tripod or shelter.
  • Use wet rawhide or paracord and tighten progressively; finish with frapping turns.
  • Test any structure with light loads before trusting it.
