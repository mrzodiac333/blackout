Scenario: Grid Collapse

A prolonged nationwide or regional power failure: what fails first, how to stay safe, and a 72-hour action plan.

Scenarios Last reviewed 2026-10-19 8 min read

India's grid is large but not invulnerable. A long outage takes out water pumping, fuel pumps, telecoms, refrigeration and traffic lights within hours. Plan for two weeks without mains power.

What fails first

  1. Traffic lights and street lighting.
  2. Water pumping and pressure.
  3. ATMs, card payments and mobile data.
  4. Fuel pumps and public transport.
  5. Refrigeration and food supply.
  6. Hospitals, unless on backup power.
Water is the first priority

Fill containers and the bathtub as soon as the power fails or an outage is announced. Water pressure may last only hours.

72-hour action plan

Hour 0–1

  • Fill all containers with water; treat before drinking.
  • Charge phones and power banks while the network still works.
  • Turn off appliances to avoid a surge when power returns.
  • Check on vulnerable neighbours.

Hour 1–6

  • Move food from the fridge to a cooler if you can; keep the fridge closed.
  • Set up lighting and radio; conserve battery.
  • Withdraw cash if ATMs work.

Day 1–3

  • Ration water and food; maintain hygiene.
  • Keep one room as a living space to conserve heat or cooling.
  • Listen to official updates; avoid rumours.
  • Use generators only outdoors, away from windows.

Specific risks

  • Carbon monoxide: generators and charcoal indoors kill. Ventilate and use CO alarms.
  • Heat: without fans or AC, watch for heat illness; use shade and wet cloths.
  • Food safety: discard refrigerated food after 4 hours without power if it is above 4 °C; when in doubt, throw it out.
  • Water contamination: assume tap water is unsafe during repairs.
  • Crime: dark streets increase opportunism; coordinate with neighbours.
  • Medication: insulin and some drugs need cold storage; use a cooler and monitor temperature.

When power returns

  • Wait for official confirmation before using the grid.
  • Turn on appliances gradually to avoid a surge.
  • Check food and water; discard anything suspect.
  • Recharge everything and restock what you used.
  • Review what worked and fix gaps.
