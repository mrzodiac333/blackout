Sewing & Gear Repair

Hand-sewing, patching, zipper and tent repair, and keeping footwear and clothing functional.

Skills & Security Last reviewed 2026-10-19 6 min read

Clothing and gear fail at the worst time. Basic repair skills keep you warm, dry and mobile — and are easy to learn now.

Hand-sewing basics

  • Running stitch: quick seams and basting.
  • Backstitch: strong seam for repairs.
  • Whip stitch: joining edges and patching.
  • Button: use a spacer (toothpick) to leave thread room.
  • Use waxed thread for canvas and outdoor gear; knot the end and finish with a few backstitches.

Outdoor gear repair

  • Tent tears: clean and dry the area, apply a patch with seam grip, and press flat.
  • Zippers: rub with candle wax or graphite; replace sliders with a kit.
  • Sleeping pads: patch with the supplied kit; clean with alcohol first.
  • Backpacks: reinforce stress points with a bar tack or heavy stitching.
  • Waterproofing: reapply DWR or seam sealant as needed.

Footwear and clothing

  • Re-glue separating soles with proper shoe adhesive; clamp overnight.
  • Replace worn laces with paracord or strong cord.
  • Patch trousers at the knee before they tear further.
  • Keep feet dry: change socks, dry boots slowly away from direct heat.
  • Layer clothing; repair insulation before cold weather.
