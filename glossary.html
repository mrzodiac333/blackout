Glossary

Plain-language definitions of survival, medical, radio and disaster terms used across this guide.

Start Here Last reviewed 2026-10-19 8 min read

Terms are grouped by topic. Use your browser's find function (Ctrl+F) to jump to a word.

Water, food and shelter

Potable
Water safe to drink. Clear water is not automatically potable.
Boil-water advisory
Official notice that tap water must be boiled before drinking, brushing teeth or washing food.
Rolling boil
Water heated until it boils vigorously; the standard disinfection method. Bring to a rolling boil for at least 1 minute at low altitude.
SODIS
Solar disinfection: clear plastic bottles filled with filtered water left in strong sun for at least 6 hours.
Greywater
Wastewater from sinks, laundry and bathing. Not safe to drink, but reusable for flushing or irrigation after treatment.
Cache
A hidden or stored supply of food, water or gear, kept away from your main home.
Bug-in / bug-out
Sheltering in place versus evacuating to another location.
Go-bag
A packed bag ready to leave with on short notice.

Medical

Triage
Sorting casualties by urgency when resources are limited.
Hemostatic dressing
A dressing containing an agent that promotes clotting in severe bleeding.
Tourniquet
A device that stops arterial bleeding on a limb by compression. Training required.
Shock
A life-threatening drop in effective blood flow. Signs: pale/cold skin, rapid weak pulse, confusion, thirst.
Sepsis
A whole-body response to infection; a medical emergency.
Debridement
Professional removal of dead or contaminated tissue from a wound. Do not attempt at home.
Oral rehydration salts (ORS)
A measured glucose-salt mix used to treat dehydration from diarrhoea.

Radio and antennas

WPC
Wireless Planning and Coordination Wing of India's Department of Telecommunications; issues amateur licences and regulates spectrum.
ASOC
Amateur Station Operator's Certificate examination.
SWR
Standing Wave Ratio: how well an antenna matches the transmitter. Lower is better; below 1.5:1 is good.
Dipole
A centre-fed half-wave antenna; a reliable baseline wire antenna.
Balun
A device matching a balanced antenna to unbalanced coax.
Simplex
Direct radio-to-radio communication without a repeater.
Repeater
A station that receives and retransmits to extend range.
ETA
Equipment Type Approval from WPC for legal sale and use of radio equipment in India.
FRS/GMRS/MURS
US licence-free or licensed services. Not legal to use in India.
PMR446
European licence-free 446 MHz service. In India, only WPC-approved low-power 446 MHz devices are permitted.

Disaster and planning

NDMA
National Disaster Management Authority, India.
NDRF
National Disaster Response Force; central specialised rescue teams.
IMD
India Meteorological Department.
Storm surge
Rise in sea level driven by a cyclone; the leading cause of cyclone deaths.
Epicentre
Point on the surface directly above an earthquake's origin.
Mains
The public electricity grid supply.
EMP
Electromagnetic pulse; can damage electronics over a wide area.
Opsec
Operational security: limiting what others can learn about your supplies, plans and movements.
