Emergency Contacts & Alerts

India emergency numbers, disaster helplines, radio and alert sources, and a printable family contact card.

Start Here Last reviewed 2026-10-19 4 min read

Save these numbers in every phone and write them on paper. In a wide-area outage, mobile data may fail before voice calls do — and both may fail before FM radio does. Program the numbers now, while you have connectivity.

For the first actions in an emergency, use Act now. For local hazard priorities, open Hazards where you live.

Life-threatening emergency

Call 112 — the national emergency response number for police, fire and ambulance. It works from mobile phones and is toll-free.

Official alerts and information

  • NDMA / Sachet — the National Disaster Management Authority runs the Sachet alert system; alerts also arrive as government SMS broadcasts. Do not disable emergency alerts on your phone.
  • India Meteorological Department (IMD) — cyclone, heatwave, heavy-rain and thunderstorm warnings at mausam.imd.gov.in. Colour codes: green (no action), yellow (be aware), orange (be prepared), red (take action).
  • INCOIS — tsunami bulletins for the Indian coast at tsunami.incois.gov.in.
  • Central Water Commission — flood forecasts at ffs.india-water.gov.in.
  • All India Radio (AIR) — the most resilient public information channel. Learn your local AIR frequencies; a battery or hand-crank FM radio is a core preparedness item.
  • District Collector / Municipal Corporation — local evacuation orders and relief camp locations. Follow them over anything in this guide.

Printable family contact card

Fill one card per household member, laminate or tape it inside a notebook, and keep a copy in every go-bag.

An out-of-area contact is someone outside your city. During local outages, long-distance calls often work when local networks are congested.

Before the network fails

  • Save numbers as contacts, but also write them on paper — phones lock, die and get lost.
  • Keep a power bank charged and a charging cable in your bag.
  • Download offline maps for your city and your evacuation route.
  • Agree on an out-of-area contact and a physical meeting point.
  • Keep ₹2,000–₹5,000 in small cash notes; ATMs and card networks fail in outages.
  • Keep a battery or hand-crank FM radio and spare cells.
