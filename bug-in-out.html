Bug In or Bug Out

A decision framework for sheltering in place versus evacuating — with triggers, thresholds and a checklist for each.

Planning Last reviewed 2026-10-19 7 min read

Most emergencies are survived by sheltering in place. Evacuation is right when the danger is worse than the road. Decide in advance, using triggers rather than panic.

The decision factors

Weigh these factors, not just fear.
FactorBug inBug out
ThreatLocalised, short, your home is safeSpreading, long, home at risk
Supplies14+ days of water, food, powerLimited or contaminated
MobilityVulnerable people can shelter safelyNeed medical care or access
RoutesRoads clear or no need to travelEvacuation advised and routes open
CommunityNeighbours can support each otherIsolation and no help nearby

Evacuation triggers

  • Official mandatory evacuation order.
  • Red IMD warning for cyclone, flood or severe storm in your area.
  • Water entering your home or rising fast.
  • Fire, gas leak, or structural damage.
  • No safe water for more than 24 hours and no way to treat it.
  • Medical emergency needing hospital care.
  • Civil unrest moving toward your area.
Leave early

Leaving before the rush is safer than leaving at the peak. If you have vulnerable people or pets, use a lower threshold.

Shelter-in-place checklist

  • Close and lock doors and windows; seal gaps if airborne hazard.
  • Fill water containers and bathtub (for washing).
  • Charge devices and power banks; set up radio.
  • Check food, medications and first-aid kit.
  • Identify the safest room and an escape route.
  • Check on neighbours; share information.
  • Keep a written log of events and decisions.

Bug-out checklist

  • Grab go-bags, documents folder, medications and cash.
  • Wear sturdy shoes and layers; carry water.
  • Tell your out-of-area contact your route and destination.
  • Lock the house; shut off utilities if instructed.
  • Take the planned route; avoid the obvious one if congested.
  • Keep the radio on for updates; avoid flood water and downed lines.
  • Check in on arrival.
