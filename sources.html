Sources & Further Reading

The standards and agencies behind this guide, plus suggested training and reading.

Start Here Last reviewed 2026-10-19 4 min read

Content is aligned with established preparedness and public-health guidance. Where Indian rules apply, WPC and NDMA material takes priority. Rules and contact numbers change; verify before relying on them.

Primary standards and agencies

  • NDMA (India) — National Disaster Management Authority guidelines on floods, cyclones, earthquakes, heatwaves and urban flooding. ndma.gov.in
  • NDRF — National Disaster Response Force operational guidance.
  • IMD — weather warnings and colour-coded alerts. mausam.imd.gov.in
  • WPC / DoT — Indian amateur radio licensing and spectrum rules. dot.gov.in
  • WHO — water safety, sanitation and outbreak guidance.
  • IFRC / Indian Red Cross Society — first aid, CPR and household preparedness.
  • FEMA / Ready.gov — household emergency planning frameworks.
  • CDC — infection control, water treatment and emergency sanitation.
  • ARRL — antenna fundamentals and amateur operating practice.
  • INCOIS — tsunami warning bulletins for India.

Hazard maps and official risk data

These are the official maps and datasets behind the state and UT hazard toolkit. They are general references; district and local conditions can differ.

  • NDMA Flood Affected Area Atlas 2023 and MoJS flood data - Historical flood-affected areas; local flash and urban flooding can exceed state totals. Official source
  • NDMA / IMD cyclone-prone districts - District-level cyclone hazard proneness for coastal states and UTs. Official source
  • BMTPC Vulnerability Atlas of India 2019 and BIS seismic zones - Seismic Zone V and IV areas are treated as a higher preparedness priority. Official source
  • India Meteorological Department heatwave guidance - Core heatwave zone and IMD heatwave criteria. Official source
  • NDMA National Landslide Hazard Atlas and BMTPC landslide incidence maps - Himalayan and Western Ghats states carry the highest landslide incidence. Official source
  • INCOIS tsunami warning and NCCR tsunami hazard maps - All coastal states and UTs are treated as tsunami-exposed. Official source
  • Forest Survey of India, India State of Forest Report 2023 - Forest-fire proneness and recent fire detections by state. Official source
  • Central Pollution Control Board non-attainment cities - Cities exceeding national ambient air-quality standards; state priorities are a simplification. Official source

In an emergency, follow IMD, NDMA, SDMA, DDMA and local officials over this site. For immediate actions, open Act now.

Training worth taking

  • Red Cross or St John Ambulance basic first aid and CPR/AED.
  • NDMA/NDRF community disaster response programmes.
  • Amateur radio ASOC (Restricted or General Grade) through WPC-recognised clubs.
  • Fire safety and extinguisher training.
  • Basic swimming and water safety.
  • Food preservation and safe canning workshops.

Accuracy and corrections

This guide is reviewed periodically and every page carries a review date. If you find an error — especially a phone number, a legal rule or a medical instruction — treat the official source as authoritative and, if you can, report the correction. The site is a static project; see the changelog for the review history.
