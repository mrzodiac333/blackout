Documents, Money & Records

Protect identity documents, keep cash and backups, and prepare for financial disruption.

Planning Last reviewed 2026-10-19 6 min read

After a disaster, paperwork determines whether you can access relief, healthcare, banking and insurance. Keep originals safe and copies accessible.

Essential documents

  • Identity: Aadhaar, PAN, passport, voter ID, driving licence.
  • Property: deeds, rent agreement, utility bills.
  • Financial: bank details, insurance policies, investment records.
  • Medical: prescriptions, vaccination records, medical history.
  • Family: birth and marriage certificates, school records.
  • Vehicle: RC, insurance, licence.

Scan everything, store encrypted copies in two places, and keep a printed set with a trusted relative.

Cash and banking

  • Keep ₹5,000–₹20,000 in small notes at home and in go-bags.
  • ATMs and card networks fail in outages; cash works.
  • Keep a small emergency fund in a separate account.
  • Photograph bank cards and keep the numbers somewhere safe — not in the same wallet.
  • Be alert to fraud and price gouging after disasters.

Insurance and relief

  • Review home, health and vehicle insurance before the monsoon season.
  • Photograph your home and belongings for claims.
  • Know how to file a claim and keep policy numbers offline.
  • Register for government relief through official channels; beware of middlemen demanding money.
  • Keep receipts for emergency expenses.

If documents are lost

  • File a police report (FIR) for lost or stolen documents.
  • Use digital copies to apply for duplicates.
  • Aadhaar and PAN can be re-downloaded or reissued through official portals.
  • Keep a list of offices and helplines for replacements.
  • After recovery, replace documents stored in damaged locations.
