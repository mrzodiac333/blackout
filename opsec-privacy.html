Opsec & Privacy

Protect your family's information, location and supplies — and avoid becoming a target during disruption.

Skills & Security Last reviewed 2026-10-19 6 min read

Operational security is about not advertising what you have or where you are. It is not paranoia; it is basic risk reduction in uncertain times.

Digital hygiene

  • Do not post real-time locations, supply hauls or evacuation plans.
  • Disable location tagging on photos and social posts.
  • Use strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication.
  • Keep offline backups of important documents.
  • Be careful with QR codes and links in forwarded messages.
  • Use encrypted messaging where appropriate; know that no system is perfect.

Physical security

  • Keep supplies out of sight from windows and visitors.
  • Do not discuss stockpiles with acquaintances.
  • Secure fuel, tools and generators.
  • Vary routines and avoid showing cash or valuables.
  • Know your neighbours; mutual trust is better than secrecy alone.

Family agreement

  • Agree what information is private: supplies, routes, meeting points, medical details.
  • Teach children not to share plans with strangers or online.
  • Use a code word to verify identity if someone claims to be sent by family.
  • Keep a list of who knows what, so information does not leak by accident.

Balance with community

Total secrecy isolates you. In a real crisis, trusted neighbours are a lifeline. Share what is necessary for mutual aid, keep the rest private, and build relationships before you need them.
