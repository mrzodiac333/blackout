Hygiene Without Running Water

Keep hands, body, laundry and living areas clean when taps, toilets and waste collection fail.

Survival Last reviewed 2026-10-19 6 min read

In disasters, disease spreads faster than anything else. Hand hygiene and safe waste management prevent more illness than any medicine you can store. Plan for hygiene as seriously as food.

Hand hygiene

Wash hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds:

  • Before preparing or eating food
  • After using the toilet or changing a child
  • After handling waste, animals or flood water
  • After coughing, sneezing or caring for a sick person

If water is scarce, use a tippy-tap: a container with a small hole and a string-operated lever that releases a trickle. Soap and a small amount of water beat hand sanitiser when hands are visibly dirty. Use alcohol sanitiser (60%+) only when hands are not visibly soiled.

Body and dental care

  • Sponge-bathe with a small amount of water; prioritise face, hands, armpits and groin.
  • Brush teeth with safe water; do not swallow.
  • Keep nails short to reduce infection and dirt.
  • Wash and dry feet daily; damp feet lead to fungal infection and trench foot.
  • Wash menstrual cloths thoroughly, dry in direct sun, and dispose of pads safely.
  • Keep clothing dry; change socks often.

Laundry and cleaning

  • Wash clothing in a bucket with a small amount of detergent, agitate, rinse, and dry in sun.
  • Prioritise underwear, socks and anything soiled.
  • Sunlight is a disinfectant; hang items in direct sun.
  • Clean high-touch surfaces (handles, switches, phones) with diluted bleach or soap.
  • Separate cleaning cloths by area; do not use the toilet cloth on dishes.

Waste and pests

  • Keep food waste in covered bins away from living areas; empty daily.
  • Bury or bag waste if collection stops; never dump near water sources.
  • Control flies and mosquitoes: screens, nets, and standing-water removal.
  • Store food in sealed containers; keep grains off the floor.
  • Wash hands after any waste handling and wear gloves if available.
