How this is classified

Each hazard is tied to an official source. The page is deliberately conservative: when in doubt, it says “watch” rather than overstating certainty.

NDMA Flood Affected Area Atlas 2023 and MoJS flood data

Historical flood-affected areas; local flash and urban flooding can exceed state totals.

Limits of this page

This is not a substitute for a district disaster management plan, a structural safety assessment or official warnings. If local authorities issue instructions, they override this page.

Read this first: This guide is for education and preparedness. It does not replace professional training, medical care, or local emergency instructions. In a real emergency, follow official guidance and call the numbers on the contacts page. Radio transmission requires a WPC licence in India; medical content is not a prescription.
