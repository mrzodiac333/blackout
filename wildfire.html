Wildfire

Prepare for forest and grass fires: defensible space, evacuation, and smoke protection.

India Hazards Last reviewed 2026-10-19 6 min read

India's forest fires peak in summer, especially in Uttarakhand, Himachal, Odisha, Madhya Pradesh and the northeast. Most are started by people. Prepare your home and leave early if fire threatens.

Defensible space

  • Clear dry grass, leaves and branches within 10–30 m of the house.
  • Prune low branches and keep trees away from the roof.
  • Store firewood, fuel and gas cylinders away from the house.
  • Keep gutters and roofs clear of debris.
  • Use fire-resistant roofing and screens where possible.
  • Keep a hose, buckets and a ladder accessible.

Evacuating

  • Leave early on official orders; do not wait to see flames.
  • Close all windows and doors; shut off gas.
  • Wear cotton or wool, not synthetics; cover your skin and face.
  • Drive with headlights on; avoid smoke-filled roads.
  • Tell your out-of-area contact where you are going.
  • If trapped in a vehicle, park away from vegetation, close vents and lie low.

Smoke and air quality

  • Stay indoors with windows closed; use a purifier or a damp cloth over vents.
  • Wear an N95/FFP2 mask outdoors; cloth masks do not filter smoke well.
  • Avoid exercise outdoors; keep children and the elderly inside.
  • Seek medical help for chest pain, severe breathlessness or wheezing.

India-specific notes

  • Forest fire season: February–June, peaking March–May.
  • Report fires to the forest department or fire services; do not attempt to fight a large fire.
  • Village fire lines and community patrols help prevent spread.
  • Smoke from fires worsens air quality across north India.
